## What's new in v1.5.0

### Units
- **Metric / imperial switch** at the top of the settings dialog — km/h ↔ mph, °C ↔ °F, litres ↔ gallons, kPa ↔ psi, mm ↔ inches. Metric stays the default, and the choice is remembered. It applies everywhere: charts, 3D HUD, corner speeds, brake analysis and the track map

### App
- **New app icon and logo**, regenerated for every platform target
- **Feedback button** in the header — explains the Discord `#bugs` and `#feedback` channels and links straight there
- **Language selector moved to the header**, next to the other buttons, so it is reachable with the race engineer panel closed
- **Race engineer panel starts collapsed** — the telemetry gets the width until you open it
- **37 more translated strings** across all 11 languages: the whole 3D viewer (sky and track conditions, weather card, tooltips), session loading, setup panel and several dialogs. A few labels were previously hard-coded German regardless of the selected language

### 3D Viewer
- **Pick which car the camera follows** — click a lap strip in the HUD; it is outlined in the lap's colour and marked with a camera icon. Defaults to the fastest lap as before
- **Porsche tail lights** are now a clean light bar that follows the rear contour, with thicker lamp units on the corners and a thin strip between them
- **Formula car rain light** sits centred and upright on the tail
- **Driving lines are a third as wide**, so several laps side by side no longer cover the asphalt where they diverge

### Fixes
- **Viewer could get stuck on "Loading 3D data…"** — changing the lap selection while a fetch was still running left the loading state on for good. Most likely with several laps selected, since those take longer to load
- **The telemetry trace now follows scrubbing.** It only advanced during playback, so dragging through the timeline moved the car but left the curve frozen. Going backwards works too
- **Gear axis shows whole gears** — the chart labelled ticks like 1.50 and 4.50, values the channel cannot take. Throttle and brake axes are cleaner for the same reason
