## What's new in v1.10.0

### An event log
An eight-minute lap of the Nordschleife is a lot of trace to read. The new
Events tab reduces a session to the handful of places where something
happened — lockups, wheelspin, excursions, missed shifts — and clicking one
shows that lap alone with the crosshair on the moment.

It also surfaces the places a driver keeps hitting: over one stint the left
rear spun up at 63.2 % of the lap in three separate laps, which no single
entry would have shown.

The thresholds were measured rather than guessed. Wheel slip below −8 %
turns up a hundred times in a GT3 stint and only twice below −12 %: the ABS
holds the wheel right around −8 to −10 %, so −12 % is where it lost the
wheel anyway. The same −12 % in a Formula Vee, which has no ABS at all,
finds twelve lockups in a quarter of an hour.

ABS engagement is listed too but starts hidden — in a car that has it there
is one engagement per braking zone, 175 over eighteen laps, which would bury
everything else. One click on its card brings it in, and the choice is
remembered.

### Setup against lap time
The setup view already put two setups side by side and marked where they
disagreed, but never said which one was faster. Each column now carries its
lap time and the gap to the best, and a filter hides every parameter the
columns agree on — a setup holds some two hundred values and usually three
of them changed.

### Driver adjustments
A new tab for what was changed from the cockpit during the run: brake bias,
ABS, traction control, throttle shape. Only the ones that actually moved are
drawn, at a fine enough threshold to catch half a percent of brake bias.

### Steering angle and ABS in General
Both channels were in every file already. Steering is scaled symmetrically
around zero, so straight ahead is the middle of the chart and a left lock
reads against a right one — auto-scaling put zero wherever the lap happened
to be lopsided.

### Fixes
- **Gaps in the sidebar were measured against a lap of another circuit.** With
  two sessions open, every Nürburgring lap showed a gap of about +373 s — to
  a 1:34 lap of Imola — and none was marked as the best. Gaps are now per
  track and car
- **The lap table marked the best time in the same colour as the selection**,
  and that colour is red, so a personal best looked like a warning. Best
  times are purple now, the way a timing screen has always done it
- **CSV export did nothing at all** — it used a browser download mechanism the
  app's webview ignores
- **Jumping to an event landed at the start line** whenever its lap was not
  already selected
- Tyre temperatures in the 3D viewer's tyre card, one figure per band
