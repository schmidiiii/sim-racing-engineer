## What's new in v1.11.0

### A G-G diagram
Lateral against longitudinal acceleration, one cloud per selected lap. The tyre
has a single grip budget to divide between turning and braking, and the shape of
the cloud is the only view in the app that shows how much of it is being spent
and how. A cross means braking, turning and accelerating happen one after
another; a filled round cloud means they overlap.

A toggle splits the picture into the nine regions — pure braking, trail braking
into a right, accelerating out of a left, and so on — and gives the share of the
lap spent in each. With a single lap selected the points take their region's
colour; comparing laps keeps the lap colours, and each region then shows one
figure per lap.

Percentiles throughout, not peaks. Raw maxima on a Nürburgring stint read 21 g
lateral and 27 g braking: those are impacts, and one crash left in the data
widens the axes until the lap itself is a dot in the middle.

### A fuel plan
Give the Laps tab a race length in laps or minutes and it says how much fuel
that takes, how many laps a tank lasts, how many stops that means and which lap
each falls on — with the lap the tank runs dry beside it, since that deadline
cannot be moved.

Two fuel figures rather than one. The average is what the stint will most likely
take, the heaviest lap is what it might, and a plan built on the average runs
dry on the heavy laps. A race given in minutes is converted at the median pace,
not the best lap, because nobody laps a whole race on their best.

### Imola and Okayama
Two more circuits with stored geometry, thirteen in total. Both were listed as
blocked by a rate limit, which was wrong: OpenStreetMap simply has little of
them — 28 % of Imola and 52 % of Okayama — and the rest falls back to the driven
line. Widened like the others they hold the car inside the drawn road for 84 %
and 82 % of the lap, against 87 % for Hockenheim.

### Driver adjustments
A tab for what was changed from the cockpit during the run: brake bias, ABS,
traction control, throttle shape. Only the ones that actually moved are drawn,
at a fine enough threshold to catch half a percent of brake bias.

### Fixes
- **ABS is drawn inside the brake trace** as an amber section, the same colour
  the 3D viewer uses, instead of a chart of its own — where the ABS cut in
  matters, a row of blips on its own does not. One shade per lap, and no lap
  colour is amber any more
- **The lap table marked the best time in the same colour as the selection**, and
  that colour is red, so a personal best looked like a warning. Best times are
  purple now
- **Working state no longer survives a restart** — column switches, a typed race
  length and hidden event kinds are where the reader was looking, not
  preferences. The theme and the unit system still persist
- **Umlauts** were missing from the German text added in the last two releases
- The setup view's lap times are gone again: they showed which setup was faster
  without showing that the setup was the reason
