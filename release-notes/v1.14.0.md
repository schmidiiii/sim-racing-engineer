## What's new in v1.14.0

### Two cars, one circuit
Laps could only be compared when both the track and the car matched. The car no
longer stands in the way: load a GT3 session and a Cup session from the same
circuit and put their laps side by side — in the delta, the traces, the track
map and the replay. Two cars round the same track share a lap distance, which
is what all of those are built on. Different circuits still cannot be compared,
because they share nothing.

In the replay, each lap now wears its own car. Until now one model was loaded
and cloned for every lap, so a Porsche selected beside a Mercedes drove round
in a Mercedes body.

The gap column in the lap list stays measured per car. A GT3 and a Cup car are
seconds apart by class, and measuring the slower one against the faster car's
best would bury what the list is for — which of *its own* laps was the good one.
Each car keeps its own best, and the delta compares them against each other.

### Choosing a session, and adding to it
The two gestures now mean two different things. Clicking a session starts from
its own two fastest laps, whatever was selected before — "show me this one".
Ticking a lap adds it to what is already there, across any session on the
circuit, in any car — "and this as well".
