## What's new in v1.8.0

### Driving line, three ways
A switch beside the camera buttons changes what the driving line shows. The
default is unchanged — each lap in its own colour.

- **PEDALS** — braking in red, throttle in green, coasting in amber. Where you
  braked and where you got back on it, without leaving the 3D view
- **DELTA** — red where the gap is opening, green where it is closing. Coloured
  by the rate, not the accumulated gap, so a mistake in the first corner does
  not paint the rest of the lap red. The reference lap keeps its own colour

### Twelve circuits with real geometry
Spielberg and Silverstone join the ten from the last release, both with real
per-point track widths. Every circuit is a little wider than before: the stored
widths run to the painted line, but a car riding the kerb is legitimately past
it, so a margin brings the drawn road closer to what is actually driveable.

### The track itself
- **Asphalt has a surface** instead of a flat fill, drawn rather than shipped —
  no licence terms, nothing added to the installer
- **Start/finish line and the starting grid**, the grid slots open towards the
  line as they are painted on a real circuit
- **Braking boards** at 300, 150, 100 and 50 m — from 300 m only where the
  corner is tight enough to deserve it, and once per corner complex rather than
  once per corner
- **Gravel traps fade into the grass** instead of ending at a hard line, and now
  begin at the road's own edge wherever it runs wide

### Light
The sun follows the session's clock, so shadows fall where they did. After dark
it becomes moonlight. The elevation is held up deliberately — following an
evening sun literally left the cars in near darkness.

### Fixes
- **The chase camera** framed the car differently while playing and paused, swung
  wildly when scrubbing, and dipped through the ground on steep sections
- **Cars hovered** above the road and floated over crests; they now sit on the
  surface with a shadow beneath them
- **Kerbs cut across the tarmac** in corners and appeared along straights
- **The driving line vanished** wherever the track climbed
- **Road markings could disappear** on longer circuits — paint lies flat on the
  road, and a few centimetres of clearance is not enough to separate them
- The lap strip under the 3D view used three different text sizes
