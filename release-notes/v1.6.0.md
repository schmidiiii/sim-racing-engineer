## What's new in v1.6.0

### 3D Viewer — car
- **Wheels turn at their own measured speed.** A locking wheel visibly stops, a spinning one races ahead, and both throw a puff of smoke once they slip more than 15% against the car
- **Tyre temperatures colour the tyres.** Black up to 55 °C, glowing redder from there to 110 °C, each corner on its own
- **Yaw, pitch and roll** are taken from the session where it logged them, so the car shows what it actually did — dive under braking, kerb strikes, the lean through a banked corner
- **Tyre HUD** with wear per corner, plus ABS and wheelspin indicators. The bottom telemetry strip gets its own ABS light
- **Wheels stand still while paused** — they used to keep turning under a stationary car

### 3D Viewer — track
- **Everything is built in metres now.** Sizes were fixed world units, which only worked while every track had a similar footprint. On the Nordschleife, six kilometres across, that meant a 152 m wide road, a 21 m long car and three times the real relief. Road, kerbs, runoff, cars, trees and terrain are all measured properly
- **The road no longer floats.** Terrain height is sampled at grid vertices up to half a cell apart — 50 m on the Nordschleife — so it could never meet the tarmac; the gap ran to 21 m. A continuous apron and verge now tie the road to the ground, and the grid is finer
- **Aprons stop where another part of the track begins.** Sections minutes apart along the lap pass within 26 m of each other with 24 m of height between them, so a wide apron sliced clean through its neighbour's tarmac
- **Runoff areas were far too big** — the sand fringe worked out 45 m wide and five metres below the road on long tracks

### Fixes
- **The app froze a moment after loading a session.** An infinite loop in the ABS marker drawing spun over a million times per frame and locked the window, so nothing responded and it could not even be closed
- **Loading no longer blocks the window.** Parsing runs off the main thread and channels load in two stages, so the app stays responsive with large files
- **New loading animation** instead of a bare line of text
- **Tyre corners were mislabelled.** The corner tags were read from each model's own orientation instead of the corrected one, so the HUD and the 3D car disagreed — a hot right-front lit up the left-rear
- **The car leaned the wrong way.** Roll is the car's attitude in the world, dominated by track banking rather than body roll, so through the Karussell it tipped away from the corner instead of into it
- **Tracks were stretched east–west** by up to 57%: a degree of longitude is shorter than a degree of latitude everywhere but the equator. This affected the track map and corner speed views as well
- **Corner geometry** no longer folds into wedges where the corner is tighter than the road is wide
