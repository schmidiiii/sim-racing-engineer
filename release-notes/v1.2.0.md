## What's new in v1.2.0

### 3D Replay Viewer
- New **3D tab** (now the default, leftmost) with a full interactive 3D replay of your lap
- **Live HUD** showing gear, speed (km/h), brake & throttle bars, steering wheel angle, and a live telemetry trace
- **TV camera mode** with dynamic FOV — automatically switches between 16 trackside positions and zooms based on distance to the car
- **Multi-lap support** — all selected laps rendered simultaneously with color-coded driving lines on the track
- **Proportional HUD scaling** — the HUD scales down gracefully when the window is smaller, keeping proportions intact
- **Delta display** per lap in the HUD (green = faster, red = slower vs. reference lap)
- **Track minimap** in the top-left corner showing live positions of all cars
