## What's new in v1.7.0

### The track is now the real track
The 3D viewer used to build the road around the line you drove, which put your
car dead centre by construction — you could never see yourself take a kerb or
run wide. iRacing's positions turn out to match the real world to within three
metres, so real geometry can be stored and used instead.

- **Ten circuits** ship with surveyed geometry: Spa (Grand Prix and Endurance),
  the VLN Gesamtstrecke, Silverstone, Road America, Road Atlanta, Hockenheim,
  Watkins Glen Classic, Summit Point and Winton. Anything else falls back to the
  old behaviour
- **Spa carries its real width**, left and right separately — the circuit runs
  from 7.4 to 16.6 m across and is up to 1.4 m wider on one side than the other
- **Everything is life size** where geometry is stored: a 9.3 m road, a 2.1 m
  car, 15 cm paint and kerbs hung off the road edge. The old figures were about
  twice life size, which is what hid the racing line in the first place

### Delta
- **A map of where the time went**, under the delta chart. The track is coloured
  by where the gap opens and closes — not by the gap itself, so a mistake in the
  first corner no longer paints the rest of the lap red
- **Hovering the chart reads out the exact delta** at that point, and marks the
  same spot on the map
- **Delta sits next to General** in the tab bar; braking and corner speed moved
  to the end

### Session
- **The driver's name** is shown, from the session's own entry rather than
  whoever happened to be first on the grid, plus the file each session came from

### Cars
- **Mercedes AMG GT3** is the default closed-wheel model. Its parts are read by
  name, so the wheels are exactly right, the rims turn without taking the tyre
  colour, and its own tail lamps light up
- Cars sit on the road with a shadow beneath them, instead of hovering 0.8 m
  above it

### Fixes
- **Accented names were unreadable.** iRacing does not write the session in one
  encoding — track names arrive as Windows-1252 and the country flair as UTF-8,
  in the same file. "Hockenheimring Baden-Württemberg" now reads correctly, as
  do driver names
- **The chase camera framed the car differently while playing and paused**, and
  swung wildly when dragging the timeline
- **Cars twitched at quarter speed** and occasionally spun a full turn: thinned
  channels were stretched by repeating values rather than interpolating, and
  interpolating an angle across the ±180° wrap sweeps it through every heading
- Kerbs no longer cut across the tarmac in corners, and no longer appear along
  straights
