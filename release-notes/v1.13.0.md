## What's new in v1.13.0

### Pick your own accent colour
The app's accent is teal now, and Settings has a colour wheel to make it
anything you like: hue round the rim, saturation out from the middle,
lightness on the slider, or type a hex code. It applies while you drag and is
remembered across restarts.

It reaches everything at once — tabs, buttons, focus rings, the marks on the
charts, the filled part of the replay timeline, and the arc around the logo,
which is repainted to match. Red is left where it means something: errors, the
brakes, and time lost.

### A free camera in the 3D replay
Hold the middle mouse button and drag: the camera comes off its rails and
swings round the car from wherever it already was, so the view never jumps.
The wheel keeps zooming. A **RESET** button appears beside the camera buttons
as soon as the mouse has moved the view, and puts it back.

### A player that lines up
The play button now sits in the middle of the viewer rather than a clock's
width to the left of it, the part of the lap already played is filled in, and
the time reads against the lap's full length.

### The sidebar reads as one thing
Laps live inside their session card instead of below it, and the two summary
strips at the bottom have become one. The best lap used to be printed twice
and the track, car and driver repeated from the card just above; what is left
describes the selection — its fastest lap, how many laps, and how consistent
they were. A session's file name shows only when another loaded session shares
its track and car, which is the case it is there for.
