## What's new in v1.12.0

### The delta sits above the traces
The General tab opens with the delta chart, above speed, throttle and brake.
It was a tab of its own, one click away from the traces that explain it — and
the question it answers, where the time went, is the question you have while
reading them.

- **One zoom for all of them.** Scroll or drag the delta and the traces
  follow, and the other way round. The two scale in different things — the
  traces in seconds into the lap, the delta in distance round it — so the
  zoom is converted through the same lap the traces draw on, and both show
  the same corner
- **Drag to pan** while zoomed in, on the delta as on the traces. Zoomed to
  a corner, the way to the next one used to be zooming out and back in
- **The same size and the same grid** as the charts under it. The delta was
  drawn to fit its container and grew with the window, standing a third
  taller than its neighbours; its gridlines now land on round values —
  a quarter of a second, half a second — with zero always on one of them
- It appears when there are two laps to compare, and stays out of the way
  when there are not

### The session line moved to the sidebar
Fastest selected lap, track, car and driver sat as four cards at the top of
the General tab, where they were read once and then scrolled past. They are
now one line above the 3D viewer, next to the consistency read-out: the time
in front, what it was set in behind it, nothing wrapping onto a line of its
own.
