## What's new in v1.4.0

### 3D Viewer — weather
- **Live track & weather panel** in the top right — track and air temperature, sky, humidity, wind, rain, surface state, rubber and time of day. Values come from the lap's own telemetry channels, so they reflect the conditions during *that* lap, with the session snapshot as fallback for older files
- **Cloud cover follows the sky** — from a handful of clouds under a clear sky up to a low, grey overcast deck
- **Rain** falls when the session logged precipitation, drifting with the actual wind direction
- **Fog, sun and sky colour** react to visibility and cloud cover; wet asphalt reads darker
- The panel collapses to a single line with a click

### 3D Viewer — cars
- **Cockpit camera (HOOD)** — a fourth view looking out over the bonnet
- **Mouse wheel zoom** in every camera mode
- **Space bar** toggles play/pause
- **Rotating wheels** with steering on the front axle, damped so they don't strobe at speed
- **Brake lights** come on under braking; the Porsche's headlights stay lit
- **Black tyres, dark glass and dark rear wing** instead of a single-colour body
- **The car now pitches with the gradient** — it noses up and down with the terrain instead of gliding level over crests

### 3D Viewer — track geometry
- **Corner artefacts fixed** — kerbs, edge lines and gravel traps no longer fold into wedges across the track in tight corners. Offsets are now limited by the local corner radius and the centreline is smoothed before anything is built from it
- **Spins no longer distort the track** — the track shape is sampled along lap distance rather than time, so a spin or an off can't tie the road into a loop. With several laps selected, the cleanest one defines the track shape

### Telemetry & comparison
- **Fuel readout** in the lap strip — live tank level plus the lap's consumption
- **Compare laps across sessions** when track and car match: matching sessions list their laps directly, and switching between them keeps your selection
- **Delta colouring** now follows the sign for every value instead of staying grey near zero

### Fixes
- The telemetry overlay no longer stays hidden after toggling laps
- Rapidly switching laps can't leave stale data on screen anymore
