## What's new in v1.9.0

### A Laps tab
A new tab beside Delta lays the whole session out one lap per row, read
in a single pass through the file.

- **Sector splits at iRacing's own sector lines**, not at even thirds. The
  number varies with the track — five at Imola, twelve on the Nordschleife —
  and they are the splits the sim shows you during a session
- **Fuel** burned and left, per lap
- **Tyres** — surface temperature, hot pressure and tread remaining, per corner
- **Driver inputs** — the share of the lap at full throttle, on the brakes,
  on neither, on both, and how often the steering changed direction
- **Track and air temperature**, so a stint that got slower has somewhere to
  point
- **Off-track excursions**, counted only where the car was off for longer
  than a wheel clipping a kerb

Above the table: pace laps, best, ideal lap, consistency and fuel per lap.
Columns are grouped and each group can be switched off — twelve sector
columns plus everything else does not fit on a screen. The whole table
exports to CSV in SI units.

### Laps that are not laps
An in-lap is driven flat out and only ends in the pits; an out-lap is not a
lap at all. The two used to be one thing. Measured on a Nürburgring stint,
the in-laps carry 3.7 s of pit road at the very end while the out-laps carry
thirty to seventy-five seconds at the start — so they are now told apart,
and only laps driven end to end count towards pace, consistency and the
ideal lap.

### Fixes
- **The delta view split every lap into even thirds** and called the pieces
  S1 to S3. It now uses the same real sector lines as the Laps tab, so the
  two views can no longer disagree about what a sector is
- **Tyre temperatures were the wrong channel** — the one iRacing only
  refreshes when the tyre is inspected in the pits. Over a whole stint it
  takes three values; the live channel takes two thousand. Temperature and
  pressure are also averaged over the lap now, because the lap ends on the
  start/finish straight where the rubber has already cooled
- **Laps iRacing never timed in full** could be selected for comparison,
  which left the delta view asking for laps that were already selected
