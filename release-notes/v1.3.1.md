## What's new in v1.3.1

### 3D Viewer — scene improvements
- **Terrain** no longer clips through the road surface — accurate floor calculation for every vertex
- **Gravel/sand runoff zones** appear at sharp corners on the outside of the kerb
- **Mountains** form a connected range surrounding the track in the far background
- **Animated clouds** drift slowly near the mountain ring; properly white in dark mode
- **Dark mode colours** for trees, kerbs, and runoff zones now match the night scene
- **Terrain & sand texture variation** — vertex colour noise gives grass and gravel organic light/dark patches
- **Track minimap** slightly larger

### UI polish
- **Consistency panel** is now compact — score, bar, Best/Spread/Ideal all in one tight row
- **Session card** is smaller — track, date, and car fit on two lines, more laps visible without scrolling
- **Telemetry trace lines** keep constant visual width at any zoom level
